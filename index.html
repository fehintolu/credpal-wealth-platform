Questions, answered

A clearer CredPal.

Here is how the platform fits together.

Is Atom part of CredPal?+

Yes. This prototype presents Atom as CredPal’s wealth and investment experience, alongside CredPal’s existing payment and credit products.

Do I have to sell my investments to access cash?+

Eligible customers may be able to access secured Naira liquidity against qualifying holdings. Terms, approval, collateral requirements, and liquidation risk apply.

What is Atom for Business?+

It is the infrastructure proposition for institutions exploring investment access, portfolio experiences, and asset-backed liquidity through APIs or white-label capabilities.

Are CredPal cards and BNPL going away?+

No. They remain part of CredPal, but sit within a broader platform focused on wealth, liquidity, business infrastructure, and everyday finance.
